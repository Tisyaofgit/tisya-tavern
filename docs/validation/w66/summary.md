## Style ownership

- Tone, viewpoint and sentence cards are optional independent world-scoped resource references. No implicit selection, destructive splitting or migration of existing styles. Original whole-style and supplement references remain available.
- Viewpoint includes female gaze, male gaze, character-oriented gaze and user-oriented gaze, plus an audience lens. user gaze is explicitly user → char; char gaze is char → user; audience gaze observes their interaction. These directional cards cannot silently invert their subject. All attention parameters remain inside viewpoint cards. These are applied creative lenses, not a universal theory or a gender/sexual-orientation assignment.
- Cards enter the existing selected-material boundary and StyleResources owner. Only selected bodies are resolved; parameter changes, saving, reopening and backup round trips are tested. Invalid role/reference bindings fail explicitly.
- Attraction can be appreciated through specific appearance, voice, competence or contrast. Desire and restraint both need evidence. Characters can initiate an invitation or approach from their own motives and respond to feedback. Player authorship and knowledge/disclosure boundaries remain authoritative.
