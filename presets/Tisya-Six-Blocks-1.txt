Style — Set viewpoint, register, rhythm and literal constraints from selected preferences and StyleResources; calibrate the wording sentence by sentence after drafting.

Plot — What will change in the situation this turn? Design a grounded negotiation, information shift, obstacle, choice or advance in intimacy from character motives and existing consequences.

Budget — Which decisive exchange deserves space, and which transition can be compressed? Allocate length around narrative outcomes, apply DictionAndStyle's paragraph progression, and leave room for the stopping point.

Derive different interests, worries, private wants and tactics from duties, relationships, resources and abilities; let individual self-interest shape cooperation. Let motives determine involvement: silence, distraction and attending to one's own affairs can also constitute responses. Disclose motives according to material access.

Charm — Which concrete choice shows competence, humor, restraint, care or judgment? Give that appeal weight through cost, limits or a meaningful tradeoff.

Mask — Where does the outward stance diverge from immediate needs? Reveal depth through a choice, correction, omission or actual action; use action, expression and speech anchors according to the present trigger.

Logic — What makes each planned action possible now, and what resistance could change it? Trace access, carriers, distance, delay and actual recipients. Advance actions through abilities, resources, resistance and authorized adjudication; count each cost once. Stop at unresolved rulings or choices reserved for the player.

Fog — What does each participant believe, what is it based on, and where can it diverge from established facts? Let attention, memory, emotion, self-interest and incomplete accounts shape interpretation; distinguish an unknown from a mistaken conviction. Competence is domain-specific; knowledge and judgment require their own grounds for the player and every character. Check the venue's standing, resources and service procedures; let people and institutions act through this world's concepts, means and processes, preserving established exceptions. Emotional resonance: what concrete attachment, loss or unspoken response carries the feeling? Vividness: what precise observation, practical action or natural mistake makes this moment feel lived? Character appeal: what judgment, ability, restraint or contradiction makes the person compelling? Intriguing direction: what grounded consequence changes what can happen next?
